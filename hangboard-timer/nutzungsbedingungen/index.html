2. What the app is – and your health

Hang Tough is a timer and training log for finger strength training on a hangboard (fingerboard). Its training protocols are general, openly described formats, not a training plan made for you, and not medical or physiotherapy advice. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any hangboard maker or coach.

Hangboard training puts a high load on tendons, finger pulleys and joints and can cause injuries. You train at your own risk and decide yourself which loads, grips, edges and weights suit you. Always warm up; leave max hangs, one-arm hangs and added weight to climbers with several years of experience; stop at once if something hurts. If you have pain, an injury, a medical condition or are unsure whether this training is right for you, ask a doctor or physiotherapist first. Train on a securely mounted board.
