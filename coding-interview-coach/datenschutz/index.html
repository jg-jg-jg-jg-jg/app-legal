Privacy policy – Coding Interview Coach

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

This policy describes what data the app “Coding Interview Coach” (“the app”) processes. In short: the app collects no personal data and has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device.

1. Controller

Johannes Groß
Afflatus
Carl-Troll-Straße 65
53115 Bonn
Germany
Email: legal@afflatus.company

2. Storage on your device

Your learning progress (which cards you rated and how, lesson progress, your streak and on how many days you opened the app), the answers in your interview profile (such as interview date, rounds, level, field, target companies, programming language, daily study time) and your settings (such as preferred practice platform, theme, reminder time) are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. “Reset progress” in the settings deletes your learning progress and streak; uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

If you have turned on Android’s backup to your Google account in your device settings, Android may include this data in that backup, so that it can be restored on a new device. This backup is your own, is run by Google under its terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and I have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

3. No connection to my own servers

The app runs no servers of its own and sends no data to me. It does not request the “Internet” permission; all cards and exercises are part of the app. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

4. Purchases

The app currently offers no purchases. If paid features are added later, payment will be handled by Google Play, and this policy will be updated first.

5. Features you start yourself

Feedback by email

“Send feedback directly to the developer” opens your email app with a prepared message to feedback@afflatus.company. Below your text it adds technical details that help me find problems: app name and build, operating system, display language, your preferred practice platform, and the number of cards learned and reviews. If you ask for a missing list from the search in free training, the message also contains your search term. You see and can edit everything before sending; nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself. I use your email address and message only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to other websites

Cards contain links, for example to similar problems on coding platforms or to sources. They open in your browser. The privacy policies of those websites apply there.

Emails to me

Emails you send to support@afflatus.company, feedback@afflatus.company or legal@afflatus.company are stored with my email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, which processes them on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The servers are in Germany; no data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Sharing

When you share an image (for example a streak or a finished lesson), the app hands it to your device’s share menu. You decide where it goes; the app itself sends nothing.

Rating

The app may ask for a rating through Google Play’s review dialog or open the store page. Google processes the rating; the app does not learn whether or how you rate it.

Reminders

Daily practice reminders are optional and scheduled on your device. For them the app uses the permissions for notifications, for device restart (to schedule the reminder again afterwards) and for vibration. No data is transmitted.

6. Google Play

When you install the app from Google Play, Google processes data under its own privacy policy. Google gives me aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs) and crash reports from users who agreed to share diagnostics with Google. These do not identify you.

7. Children

The app is meant for people preparing for job interviews. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to legal@afflatus.company. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

9. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, I will update this policy first. The version published here applies.
