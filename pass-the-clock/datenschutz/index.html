Privacy policy – Pass the Clock: Turn Timer

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

This policy describes what data the app “Pass the Clock: Turn Timer” (“the app”) processes. In short: the app collects no personal data and has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your players, games and settings stay on your device.

2. Storage on your device

The player names and colours you enter, the current setup, saved groups, a game in progress, your finished games (playing times per player, for the history and statistics), your settings (such as sounds, language, theme) and whether Pro is unlocked are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it; the history can also be deleted in the app.

3. No connection to my own servers

The app runs no servers of its own and sends no data to me. The clock runs entirely on your device. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. On Android it uses the permission for vibration (a short buzz when the clock passes on and when time is up), the camera only for scanning a QR code (see below), and keeps the screen on during a game.

4. Pro purchase through Google Play or the App Store

The app offers a one-time purchase (“Pro”). The purchase and payment are handled entirely by the store you install the app from, which is responsible for them: Google Play on Android, Apple’s App Store on iPhone. I receive no payment details. On your device, the app only asks the store whether you own Pro, to unlock the Pro features. Google provides me with order details (such as order number, country, price); Apple provides me with sales reports (such as country, price and number of purchases) without your name or contact details. I use them only for accounting and tax. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy applies.

5. Features you start yourself

Feedback by email

“Send feedback to the developer” opens your email app with a prepared message to feedback@afflatus.company. Below your text it adds technical details that help me find problems: app version, operating system, display language and whether Pro is unlocked. You see and can edit everything before sending; nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself. I use your email address and message only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails to me

Emails you send to support@afflatus.company, feedback@afflatus.company or legal@afflatus.company are stored with my email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, which processes them on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The servers are in Germany; no data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Sharing

When you share, you choose what: an image of the game’s result (the game’s name, the players’ names, times, winners and points) or a picture recommending the app. The app hands the image and a short text with the link to the app’s store page to your device’s share menu. You decide where it goes; the app itself sends nothing.

Transferring the history

You can pass your finished games to another phone without any server: the app packs them (game names, players’ names, times, winners and points) into a code that you send yourself, for example with a messenger, or shows it as a QR code. Importing a code you received adds its games on your device or replaces your history, as you choose. To scan a QR code the app uses the camera with your permission; the picture is read on your device only and is neither stored nor sent.

Rating

The app may ask for a rating through the store’s review dialog (Google Play or the App Store) or open the store page. The store operator (Google or Apple) processes the rating; the app does not learn whether or how you rate it.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to legal@afflatus.company. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

8. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, I will update this policy first. The version published here applies.
