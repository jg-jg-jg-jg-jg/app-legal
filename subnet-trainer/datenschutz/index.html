Privacy policy – Subnet Trainer: Daily Drills

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

This policy describes what data the app “Subnet Trainer: Daily Drills” (“the app”) processes. In short: the app has no account and no servers of my own, and your learning progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; for them, Google processes data about your device, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only as far as you agree. With Pro, the app shows no ads.

2. Storage on your device

Your progress (solved problems, streaks, the free problems used in each exercise), your settings (such as language, theme, reminder time), whether Pro is unlocked and the counters that decide when the next ad is due are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. No connection to my own servers

The app runs no servers of its own and sends no data to me. The exercises are created and checked on your device. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. It uses the “Internet” permission only for the ads and the consent form (section 4), for the Pro purchase through Google Play (section 5) and, right before an ad is due, for a short online check: the app calls connectivitycheck.gstatic.com, the address of Google that Android itself uses to test its connection. Like every request on the internet, this hands Google your IP address; the app sends nothing else with it.

4. Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

Between exercises, the free version shows full-screen ads. They come from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”), through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK collects data and sends it to Google, encrypted: your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), interactions with the app and the ads (such as app starts, taps and ad views), diagnostic data, information about your device, and device identifiers – the advertising ID and the app set ID (Google’s description). Google also uses this data for its own purposes and is responsible for that itself; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before it requests the first ad. There you decide whether Google and its advertising partners may store and read information on your device and show you personalised ads. If you do not agree, you see non-personalised or limited ads instead; Google then uses data only as far as it needs to deliver and count the ad and to prevent fraud.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → “Privacy settings for ads” (the entry appears where the consent form applies). A withdrawal does not affect what was done before it. In your device’s settings (on many devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads) you can reset or delete the advertising ID. In My Ad Center you manage how Google personalises ads for you.

Legal basis

Storing and reading information on your device and personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Showing non-personalised or limited ads, counting them and preventing fraud: my legitimate interest in financing the free version through ads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Transfer outside the EU

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses the EU standard contractual clauses (Google’s data transfer frameworks).

With Pro

Once Pro is unlocked, the app requests no more ads, shows no consent form and makes no online check.

5. Pro purchase through Google Play

The app offers a one-time purchase (“Pro”): no ads, and every exercise without limits. The purchase and payment are handled entirely by Google Play, which is responsible for them. I receive no payment details. On your device, the app only asks Google Play whether you own Pro, to unlock it. Google provides me with order details (such as order number, country, price); I use them only for accounting and tax. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The Google Privacy Policy applies.

6. Features you start yourself

Feedback by email

“Send feedback directly to the developer” opens your email app with a prepared message to feedback@afflatus.company. Below your text it adds technical details that help me find problems: app version, operating system, display language, whether Pro is unlocked, the number of solved problems and your best streak. You see and can edit everything before sending; nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself. I use your email address and message only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails to me

Emails you send to support@afflatus.company, feedback@afflatus.company or legal@afflatus.company are stored with my email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, which processes them on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The servers are in Germany; no data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Sharing

When you share an image (for example a streak milestone), the app hands it to your device’s share menu. You decide where it goes; the app itself sends nothing.

Rating

The app may ask for a rating through Google Play’s review dialog or open the store page. Google processes the rating; the app does not learn whether or how you rate it.

Reminders

Daily practice reminders are optional and scheduled on your device. For them the app uses the permissions for notifications, for device restart (to schedule the reminder again afterwards) and for vibration. No data is transmitted.

7. Children

The app and its ads are not directed at children under 13.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to legal@afflatus.company. For the data Google processes for ads, you can also contact Google directly. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

9. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, I will update this policy first. The version published here applies.
