Privacy policy – UltraReady: Prepare for Aon

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

This policy describes what personal data the app “UltraReady: Prepare for Aon” (“the app”) and its store link page process, for what purpose, on what legal basis and with whom. In short: the app has no account and no servers of my own. Your training plan and your results stay on your device. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. Data goes to third parties only where you start it yourself (a purchase, a video, an email, sharing) or where the store you installed the app from processes it (Google or Apple).

UltraReady is an independent practice app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or reviewed by Aon plc or its subsidiaries. “Aon” is a trademark of Aon plc and is used only to name the tests the app prepares for.

2. Storage on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • your training plan: the test types you chose, your test date, the time per day and the reminder time;
  • your results: for each test you took, your answers, the time per task and the date (the latest 300 attempts);
  • your settings: the language, whether you confirmed the notice at the first start;
  • whether a Pro pass is active and until when, and the free simulations and videos used;
  • the counters that decide when the app may show a hint by itself: on how many days you used the app, when it last asked for a rating or offered reminders, which milestones it congratulated you on, how often you shared a task.

This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. From it the app computes your statistics, the readiness index and the training plan, all on your device. Storing it is strictly necessary to provide the app you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); the legal basis for using it is the provision of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). It stays until you delete the app or its data in your device’s settings.

3. No connection to my own servers

The app runs no servers of its own and sends no data to me. The app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. If that ever changes, I will update this policy first and ask for your consent in the app where it is required.

Permissions: on Android the app asks for permission to show notifications (reminders, section 8) and starts at device restart only to schedule the reminders again; on iPhone it asks for permission to send notifications. Internet access is used only for the optional videos (section 4) and for purchases through the store (section 5).

4. Optional videos in the free version (Google AdMob)

In the free version you can choose to watch a short video ad to unlock one more simulation. The app shows no other ads: no banners, no ads between tasks or after a simulation. The videos come from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”), through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK collects data and sends it to Google, encrypted: your IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), interactions with the app and the ads (such as app starts, taps and ad views), diagnostic data, information about your device, and device identifiers – on Android the advertising ID and the app set ID, on iPhone the identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking (below), the advertising identifier (IDFA) (Google’s description for Android and for iPhone). Google also uses this data for its own purposes and is responsible for that itself; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before it requests the first video. There you decide whether Google and its advertising partners may store and read information on your device and show you personalised ads. If you do not agree, you see non-personalised or limited ads instead; Google then uses data only as far as it needs to deliver and count the ad and to prevent fraud.

On iPhone, before the first video, Apple also asks whether the app may track you across apps and websites of other companies (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you tap “Allow” does the advertising identifier (IDFA) go to Google; otherwise the ads are shown without it. You can change this at any time on your iPhone under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under “Privacy settings for ads” in the info dialog (the entry appears where the consent form applies). A withdrawal does not affect what was done before it. In your device’s settings (on many Android devices under Settings → Privacy → Ads) you can reset or delete the advertising ID. In My Ad Center you manage how Google personalises ads for you.

Legal basis

Storing and reading information on your device, personalised ads and, on iPhone, tracking: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Showing non-personalised or limited ads, counting them and preventing fraud: my legitimate interest in financing the free version through the videos you choose to watch (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

With Pro

While a Pro pass is active, the app requests no videos and shows no consent form or tracking question.

5. Pro passes through Google Play or the App Store

The app offers Pro passes for a fixed period (one-time purchases, no subscription, no automatic renewal). The purchase and payment are handled entirely by the store you install the app from, which is responsible for them: Google Play on Android, Apple’s App Store on iPhone. I receive no payment details. On your device, the app only asks the store whether you own a pass, to unlock it. Google provides me with order details (such as order number, country, price); Apple provides me with sales reports (such as country, price and number of purchases) without your name or contact details. I use them only to process the purchase and for accounting and tax, and keep them as long as tax law requires (up to ten years). The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). The Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy applies.

6. Statistics from Google Play and the App Store

Google and Apple provide me with statistics about the app in their developer consoles (Google Play Console, App Store Connect): for example the number of installs, ratings and, from devices whose owners agreed to share diagnostic data with app developers, crash and performance reports. They are aggregated or contain no names or contact details. I use them only to find and fix errors. The stores collect this data under their own responsibility; my legitimate interest in a stable app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) is the legal basis for looking at it. You decide in your device’s settings whether diagnostic data is shared (on iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → “Share with App Developers”).

7. Features you start yourself

Feedback and error reports by email

“Send feedback” and, on the answers page, “Report an error” open your email app with a prepared message to feedback@afflatus.company. Below your text it adds technical details that help me find problems: app name and build, operating system, display language, whether Pro is active and which test you took last (its internal name, such as “verbal/brakmoor”); an error report also names the task (such as “s11”). You see and can edit everything before sending; nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself. I use your email address and message only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that, at the latest after two years. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails to me

Emails you send to support@afflatus.company, feedback@afflatus.company or legal@afflatus.company are stored with my email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, which processes them on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The servers are in Germany; no data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Sharing

When you share an image (a result, a milestone or a task as a puzzle), the app first shows it to you and then hands it, with a short text and the link to the store link page (section 9), to your device’s share menu. The images contain no personal data: no name, no test date, no history. You decide where they go; the app itself sends nothing. The service you share with (such as a messenger) is responsible for what it does with them.

Rating

The app may ask for a rating through the store’s review dialog (Google Play or the App Store), and “Rate the app” opens the store page. The store operator (Google or Apple) processes the rating; the app does not learn whether or how you rate it.

Links to other websites

Some pages of the app link to other websites, for example to Aon’s public preparation pages, to the store pages or to this policy. They open in your browser. The app passes nothing on; the operator of the website you open is responsible for it.

Daily training reminders are optional and scheduled on your device; the texts (such as the countdown to your test) are created on your device. No data is transmitted.
