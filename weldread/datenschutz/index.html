Privacy policy – WeldRead: Read Welding Symbols

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Deutsche Fassung weiter unten

This policy describes what data the app “WeldRead: Read Welding Symbols” (“the app”) processes. In short: the app collects no personal data and has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device.

1. Controller

Johannes Groß

Germany
Email: legal@afflatus.company

2. Storage on your device

Your progress (learned symbols and their review schedule, experience points, streak, mock exam results), your settings (such as language, drawing standard, daily goal, exam date, reminder time) and whether Pro is unlocked are stored only on your device. The app never sends this data to me or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it from your device.

If you have turned on Android’s backup to your Google account in your device settings, Android may include this data in that backup, so that it can be restored on a new device. This backup is your own, is run by Google under its terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and I have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

3. No connection to my own servers

The app runs no servers of its own and sends no data to me. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. Purchases and the rating dialog are handled by the store app on your device (see sections 4 and 5).

4. Pro purchase through Google Play

The app offers Pro as a subscription (yearly or monthly) or as a one-time purchase (“One-time purchase”). The purchase, payment, renewal and cancellation are handled entirely by Google Play, which is responsible for them. I receive no payment details. On your device, the app only asks Google Play whether you own Pro, to unlock the content. Google provides me with order details (such as order number, country, price); I use them only for accounting and tax. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The Google Privacy Policy applies.

5. Features you start yourself

Feedback by email

“Send feedback” and the link “I found a mistake in the app”, shown after a wrong answer, open your email app with a prepared message to feedback@afflatus.company. Below your text it adds technical details that help me find problems: app version, operating system and its version, device manufacturer and model, display language, drawing standard and whether Pro is unlocked; when you report a question, also that question with its answer options in the order shown, the correct answer, the one you chose and a block of technical data that describes the question and its pictures, so it can be rebuilt exactly. You see and can edit everything before sending; nothing is sent unless you tap “Send” yourself. I use your email address and message only to answer you and improve the app, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in handling your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emails to me

Emails you send to support@afflatus.company, feedback@afflatus.company or legal@afflatus.company are stored with my email provider STRATO GmbH, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany, which processes them on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The servers are in Germany; no data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Sharing

When you share an image (for example a symbol puzzle or a success such as a streak), the app creates it on your device and hands it to your device’s share menu. You decide where it goes; the app itself sends nothing.

Rating

The app may ask for a rating through Google Play’s review dialog or open the store page. Google processes the rating; the app does not learn whether or how you rate it.

Reminders

Practice reminders are optional and scheduled on your device. For them the app uses the permissions for notifications and for device restart (to schedule the reminder again afterwards). No data is transmitted.

Home-screen widget

If you place the app’s widget on your home screen, it shows your streak and the reviews that are due, read from the data on your device. No data is transmitted.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to legal@afflatus.company. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.

8. Right to object

Where I process data on the basis of my legitimate interest (the feedback emails in section 5), you may object to this processing at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. An informal email to legal@afflatus.company is enough.

9. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, I will update this policy first. The version published here applies.
